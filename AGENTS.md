# Project rules

- Keep the summer launch teaser off the homepage after launch; its embedded film end card describes a future summer launch and cannot be corrected with surrounding copy alone.
- Launch-copy updates must preserve contact submission, analytics identifiers and pricing calculations; a change in operational status does not imply changes to these flows.
- Public sizing tools expose only sizing via system-sizing; legacy pricing calculations remain internal until the owner approves a viable price.
- Agent-facing solar estimates use the same public sizing projection as the visible calculator, not the retired cost-analysis response.
- Share dated municipal financing facts through solar-facts; do not represent financing as a rebate or approval.
- Generate sitemap article routes from the article data, not a separate maintained slug list; removed posts receive per-route noindex in this SPA.
